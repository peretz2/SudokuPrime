Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I play Sudoku?

A: Fill the 9×9 grid so that each row, column, and 3×3 box contains the numbers 1-9 without repetition. Tap an empty cell, then tap a number from the number pad to place it. Use the tutorial in Settings for a complete guide.

Q: What are the difficulty levels?

A: Sudoku Prime offers four difficulty levels:

  • Easy: Perfect for beginners, more starting numbers
  • Medium: Requires some strategy and logic
  • Hard: For experienced players, fewer hints
  • Expert: Maximum challenge for Sudoku masters

Q: How do Notes work?

A: Tap the Notes button (pencil icon) to enable Notes mode. Then tap a cell and select multiple numbers to mark possibilities. Notes appear as small numbers in the cell. Tap the Notes button again to return to normal mode.

Q: What is the 3-mistake rule?

A: You can make up to 3 mistakes per game. The mistake counter (❌ X/3) shows your progress. On the 3rd mistake, the game ends with a Game Over screen. You can then restart the same puzzle or return to the menu.

Q: How do Hints work?

A: Hints provide the next logical move with an explanation. You get 1 free hint per day. Additional hints can be purchased:

  • 3 Hints - $1.99
  • 10 Hints - $4.99
  • Unlimited Hints - $9.99 (one-time purchase)

Q: What is the Daily Challenge?

A: A new puzzle every day that's the same for all players worldwide. Complete it to build your daily streak! Streaks are tracked in Statistics.

Q: How do I restore my purchases?

A: Go to Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases. This will restore any non-consumable purchases (Unlimited Hints). Note: Consumable hints (3-pack and 10-pack) cannot be restored as per App Store policy.

Q: Can I pause the game?

A: Yes! Tap the pause button next to the timer. The game also auto-pauses when you exit the app. Tap Resume to continue.

Q: How do I resume a saved game?

A: If you have an unfinished game, tap "Resume Game" on the main menu. The app automatically saves your progress.

Q: Does the app work offline?

A: Yes! Sudoku Prime works completely offline. All puzzles are generated on your device. No internet connection required.

Q: Are there ads?

A: No! Sudoku Prime - No Ads is completely ad-free. Enjoy uninterrupted gameplay with no distractions.

Q: Can I change the theme?

A: Yes! Go to Settings → Appearance → Color Theme. Choose from Classic Blue, Dark Pro, or Colorful themes.

Q: How do I turn off sound or haptics?

A: During gameplay, tap the menu button (three dots) in the top right → toggle Sound or Haptic. You can also adjust these in Settings.

Q: Where are my statistics stored?

A: All statistics are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to any server. If you uninstall the app, your statistics will be lost.

Q: Can I request a refund?

A: All purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. To request a refund, visit Apple's refund support page or contact Apple Support directly.

Technical Support

If you're experiencing technical issues:

  1. Make sure you're running the latest version of Sudoku Prime - No Ads (check App Store for updates)
  2. Restart the app
  3. Restart your device
  4. If the issue persists, contact us at jaccoperetz@gmail.com
